# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Boundless is a small public project. Participation is expected to stay respectful, technical, and constructive.

Examples of behavior that contribute to a productive environment:

- assuming good intent while discussing technical tradeoffs
- giving actionable, evidence-based feedback
- respecting different experience levels and communication styles
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- discriminatory language or hateful conduct
- doxxing or sharing private information without permission
- repeated bad-faith arguments, trolling, or deliberate disruption
- sexualized language or imagery in project spaces

## Scope

This applies to project spaces and project-related communication, including issues, pull requests, code review, and other public or private interactions tied to the repository.

## Reporting

Do not open a public issue for conduct concerns.

Report serious or sensitive incidents privately through the contact route on the maintainer's GitHub profile: [github.com/bestlux](https://github.com/bestlux).

Include links, screenshots, or other concrete evidence when possible.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or block participation for behavior that violates this code of conduct.

Responses will be proportional to the severity and pattern of the behavior, with the goal of protecting contributors and keeping the project usable.
